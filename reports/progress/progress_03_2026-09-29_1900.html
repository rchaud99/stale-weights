Stale Weights: progress report 03

2026-09-29, 19:00 EDT · the transfer check · Qwen2.5-14B, seeds 0–1, two domains
Where things stand. The second domain (a retail return policy) is complete for two seeds, and the preregistered transfer check is in. The headline transfers only in part. For the strict old policy (A: a 14-day window, stricter than the model's ~30-day prior), the omitted window is again filled from the weights despite "apply only the rules written in this policy". The fill is large (+32 to +50 points over the same-data control; 4/4 tests, Holm p ≤ 1e-11). A's categorical final-sale rule fills weakly: significant on seed 1 (+12–13 points), not on seed 0. For the lenient old policy (B: 60 days), there is no decision effect in either rule type (0/8), even though B-tuned still cites its old 60-day window in most of its reasons. Stating the removal explicitly fixes the fill in both domains. A change notice is not a reliable fix in either. So the honest headline is narrower than "fine-tunes leak into gaps": it held in decisions for 3 of the 4 policy × domain arms (airline A and B, returns A) and failed for returns B. It is not simply "restrictive leaks, lenient doesn't": airline B (48 h, more lenient than the 24 h prior) did fill. The failing arm is also the least deny-heavy training set (41% denials). n = 2 domains, 1 model.
